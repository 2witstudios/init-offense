Converge loop

Carry the entire owner-authorized outcome to completion. A builder may request independent branch feedback, implement verified fixes and request delta review without owner relays or a review pass limit. Use native pu spawn/send/status/logs; code-writing children use separate worktrees and reviewers remain read-only.

Keep one durable continuation record: candidate, remaining work, provisional producer lineage and proof obligations. Intermediate CI can fail; defer a check explicitly with reason, responsible agent and discharge point. Continue work that can progress and rerun only when relevant inputs change. Main acceptance requires the completed composed candidate, all applicable gates and independent exact-head review. Production identity, secret, data and deployment sign-offs remain human-only.

Do not declare a smaller passing subset complete, silently change criteria or abandon in-scope findings as unspecified follow-ups. Escalate changed product intent or an actual protected/conflicting action; do not repeat routine approvals or acknowledgments. Stop a review hold when the reviewer is no longer using the mutable worktree, and preserve historical review results.
